The Inflection Point

The next 75 years won't look like the last.

Today there are 11,767 of them practicing in the state, and the work has never mattered more.

What's changed is everything around the work. Most clinicians now lose hours a day to documentation, billing, and prior auths. None of that is what they trained for, and the tools that were supposed to help haven't really kept up. Burnout is rising, and the reason is the system around the work, not the work itself.

That's the gap SPRY and OPTA are working to close together.
