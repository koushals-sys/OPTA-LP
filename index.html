75
The Inflection Point

Physical therapy
is at a turning point.

For 75 years, Ohio's physical therapists built something that outlasted every shift in medicine, every policy battle, every change in how care gets delivered. Today, 11,767 licensed physical therapists practice in Ohio, and the profession has never been more necessary.

But most clinicians spend a significant part of their day on work they didn't train for. Documentation. Billing. Prior authorizations. The administrative burden has grown faster than the tools designed to carry it, and burnout is rising. Not because the work is wrong, but because the system around it is.

AI is arriving with promises. The ones worth paying attention to disappear into the background, handling the paperwork so clinicians never have to choose between the patient and the screen. That is the question OPTA's 75th anniversary is asking. And the only one worth answering well.
